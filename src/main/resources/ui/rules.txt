Rules:
Xtreme Tasker adds extra rules on top of official Tasker. See the GitHub README.

Boss combat training allowance
For any task requiring that you kill a boss with a suggested skills section on their "strategies" OSRS wiki page, you are allowed to train your combat skills to those suggested skills. You must do this through the Slayer skill, with any slayer master(s) of your choosing.

It's heavily recommended to be strategic when choosing your slayer master(s) for supplies and equipment throughout the grind. For example, Krystilia's slayer list includes mammoths which drop single dose prayer potions. This would be especially useful for bosses that require overhead prayers to kill them.

Official Tasker rules
Xtreme Tasker is built on top of official Tasker, a well established ruleset developed by the Tasker community. All official Tasker rules apply in full — refer to the Rules and Overview section of the TaskerFAQ for all tasks, including combat achievements.
