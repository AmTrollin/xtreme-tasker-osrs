# Xtreme Tasker Rules

Xtreme Tasker builds on the official Tasker ruleset and expands the challenge with Combat Achievements, Collection Log goals, and Achievement Diaries.

All official Tasker rules apply unless an Xtreme Tasker rule below says otherwise.

## Official Tasker Rules

Xtreme Tasker uses the official Tasker ruleset as its foundation. For the base rules, progression expectations, and general task restrictions, refer to the Rules and Overview section of the TaskerFAQ:

## Xtreme Tasker Additions

### Expanded Task Sources

Xtreme Tasker rolls from three task sources:

- Combat Achievements
- Collection Log slots
- Achievement Diaries

The active Roll Source plugin setting controls whether you roll from every source, Combat Achievements only, or Collection Logs and Achievement Diaries only.

### Combat Achievement Tiers

Combat Achievements follow their in-game tier placement, with one plugin specific adjustment:

- Grandmaster Combat Achievements are included in the Master tier.

### Boss Combat Training Allowance

For any task requiring that you kill a boss with a suggested skills section on that boss's strategies page on the OSRS Wiki, you may train your combat skills up to those suggested skill levels.

This training must be done through Slayer. You may choose any slayer master or combination of slayer masters.

It is heavily recommended to be strategic when choosing slayer masters for supplies and equipment throughout the grind. For example, Krystilia's slayer list includes mammoths, which can drop single dose prayer potions. That can be useful for boss tasks that require overhead prayers.

### Account Sync

Xtreme Tasker can sync supported account progress into the plugin, but sync exists to catch already completed progress. It does not change the rules for how tasks should be completed after they are rolled.

Collection Log syncing only includes entries RuneLite has seen. For best results, open your in-game Collection Log before syncing.

Sync results are staged for review before they are applied.
